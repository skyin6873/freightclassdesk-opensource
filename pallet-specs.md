# Pallet specification tables

Extracted from published freight/pallet references on Freight Class Desk (retrieved 2026-11-10).

## GMA pallet

- Dimensions: 48 in long x 40 in wide.
- Height: 5 to 6.5 in (the 6.5 in step is what can move the density class).
- Rated load: at least 2,500 lb.
- Empty weight: 33 to 49 lb.
- Body: Grocery Manufacturers Association (now Consumer Brands Association) standard wood pallet.

Source: https://freightclassdesk.com/gma-pallet/

## Empty pallet weights by type

- Standard 48x40 wood stringer pallet: 30-48 lb empty.
- EPAL 1 Euro pallet: about 48 lb.
- Rackable plastic pallet: 40-55 lb.

Source: https://freightclassdesk.com/pallets-weight/
